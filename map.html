Visit Us at Zoo Everwild

Nestled in a vibrant corner of the city, Zoo Everwild is a place where nature and adventure meet. Our gates open to lush pathways, gentle breezes, and the calls of exotic animals from every corner of the globe.

We are easy to find — just minutes from the town centre — with convenient parking, public transport links, and clear signposts guiding you straight to our entrance. Whether you’re coming for a family day out, a school trip, or a quiet stroll among the wildlife, our location makes it simple to escape into the wonders of the animal kingdom.

Address: University street 60 | Opening Hours: 9:00 - 20:00
